Choose an agency by the work you need done

A Marketing agency City of Oswego business owners consider should be able to explain how its work connects to your day-to-day needs. A short checklist before you hire

Use these questions to compare options and prepare for a useful conversation:

  • Which service or product do you want to bring more attention to?
  • Where do prospective customers first contact your business?
  • Who responds, and what happens if a message arrives outside your usual workflow?
  • Are your website and business profile details accurate?
  • Do you have a way to keep in touch with a prospect who isn't ready yet?

Watch for a plan that promises activity without asking how your business handles a new inquiry. Another common mistake is adding automation before deciding what a good response should say. Have examples of your current contact process ready, and point out where leads are being missed or follow-up feels inconsistent. That gives the discussion a concrete starting point.
