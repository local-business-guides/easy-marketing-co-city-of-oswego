What to prepare and what the work may include

If you have access to your website or Google Business Profile, be ready to discuss who manages them.

Depending on the need, Nonprofit Digital Marketing City of Oswego may include:

- Website development or improvements to your existing site
- Google Business Profile optimization and reputation management
